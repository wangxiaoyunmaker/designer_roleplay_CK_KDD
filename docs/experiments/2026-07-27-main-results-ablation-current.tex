\subsection{Main Results}
\label{sec:main_results}

Tables~\ref{tab:main_results_current} and
\ref{tab:main_value_results_current} report trajectory-level personalization
and V30 value alignment, respectively. Generic prompting frequently
converges to a small set of population-level chains. On Qwen3-8B, SP produces
only one sequence for all 162 participants, whereas our method produces 50
unique sequences and reduces TopShare from \(1.0000\) to \(0.1173\). It also
achieves the highest KCDR (\(0.5472\)) and BGE own-user margin (\(0.0035\)).
The same anti-collapse pattern holds on GPT-4o: our method yields 132 unique
sequences, lowers TopShare to \(0.0309\), and attains the highest KCDR
(\(0.5904\)). These results show that the proposed conditioning scheme
substantially reduces convergence to a dominant population-level trajectory.

At the value level, our method obtains the highest own-profile advantage on
Qwen3-8B (\(\mathbf{0.0432}\)) and a comparably positive result on
Llama-3.1-8B (\(0.0485\), versus \(0.0491\) for CoSER). It also achieves the
highest profile-polarity precision on Qwen3-8B
(\(\mathbf{0.5264}\)). These results indicate that the increased trajectory
diversity is not merely arbitrary variation: generated responses remain
preferentially aligned with the intended participant's value profile.

The BGE result is backbone-dependent, but the trajectory-level evidence is
consistent across Qwen3-8B and GPT-4o: our method expands the support of
generated C--K trajectories while reducing the frequency of the dominant
chain. We therefore interpret the main gain as resistance to population-level
mode collapse.

\subsection{Ablation Analysis}
\label{sec:ablation_analysis}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation_current} isolates the components most relevant to this
effect. Removing Q2 increases TopShare from \(0.0309\) to \(0.0617\) and
reduces Unique from 132 to 125, showing that participant history helps
differentiate trajectories. Removing the learned rules reduces KCDR from
\(0.5904\) to \(0.5736\), indicating that rule-guided routing preserves more
of the between-participant variation found in the human trajectories.
Finally, adding Full30 provides no KCDR gain over active4-only
(\(0.5852\) vs.\ \(0.5904\)), supporting the more compact active-trait
representation.
